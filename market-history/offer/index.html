NJ market history — the tools

Three years of public New Jersey sale records — deeds, listings and market aggregates — hydrated, deduped and cleaned into one dataset, then turned into a few tools for pricing and browsing houses around Westfield. Everything below reads the same numbers, so they can't disagree.

  1. 01 Price a single house — what an ask is worth open → Paste one house's beds, baths, size and asking price and see what comparable local sales say it's worth — seasonally adjusted, with every comp shown and every assumption flagged. Thin towns now borrow from their neighbours.
  2. 02 On the market — and what it's worth open → Browse what's for sale right now across the target towns, each listing priced against comparable sales, with a housing score. A dated snapshot, not a live feed — it says how old it is at the top.
  3. 03 The map — by the numbers open → Every town on a map, coloured by price, schools, commute, appreciation and flood risk — with the live listings dropped on top as points.
  4. 04 Every sale — what houses actually went for open → Search the whole dataset of closed sales. Public record, so unlike the market page it can't go stale.

Built from public-record NJ sale data. Not a valuation, and never advice — a way to argue with an asking price using what similar houses actually sold for.
