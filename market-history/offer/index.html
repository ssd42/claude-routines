The house hunt — Westfield & around

Five tools, built on three years of real New Jersey sale records.

  • Price a house→ Is an asking price fair? Check one house against similar local sales.
  • What's for sale→ Everything on the market now, each priced against comparable sales.
  • Compare towns→ A map of every town — prices, schools, taxes, commute, flood risk.
  • What sold→ Search 40,000 closed sales and see what houses actually went for.
  • How right are we?→ Every house that sold this year, priced by our own tool with the house hidden from itself — next to what it really went for.
Public-record NJ sale data — not a valuation. A way to argue with an asking price using what similar houses actually sold for.
