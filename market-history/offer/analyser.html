Price comps against

Enter a town and an asking price. Add sq ft, beds and baths and it will also pull comparable sales.

Two numbers get computed, and they are deliberately kept apart:

  • Comps — what similar homes actually sold for. Filtered by size; pooled across all months.
  • The seasonal factor — what the market does to an asking price in a given month. Every house type; one town.

Sliced together they'd bottom out at one sale per bucket. Kept apart, each rests on 15–30.
