NJ SCHOOL RATINGS — 2024-25
===========================
school_ratings.csv — one row per target ZIP (57 of 57; Westfield 07090 has no row).

SOURCE
  NJ DOE Statewide Assessment Results 2024-25
  https://www.nj.gov/education/assessment/results/reports/2425/

WHAT THE RATING IS
  `*_rating_1_to_10` = a custom NJ statewide DISTRICT decile. 1 = bottom 10% of NJ
  districts, 10 = top 10%. Composite proficiency is weighted by valid test scores.
  Three levels ride separately: elementary, middle, high_school.

THE CAVEAT THAT MATTERS — IT IS A DISTRICT PROXY, NOT A CATCHMENT
  The rating is attached to a ZIP via the town label. Schools are assigned by
  ATTENDANCE BOUNDARY, not by ZIP, and the two do not agree:
    * one ZIP can span districts AT DIFFERENT LEVELS — Garwood's elementary is
      Garwood Boro but its high school is Clark Township;
    * a large township (Woodbridge: Colonia / Avenel / Iselin) has one label here
      and several catchments in reality;
    * two houses on the same street can feed different elementary schools.
  So this is a TOWN-level signal. For an actual house, verify the boundary. The file
  says so itself in `assignment_note`, and that note is not boilerplate.

WHY THIS AND NOT layers/education/
  `education/` is ACS B15003 — the share of ADULTS 25+ holding degrees. That measures
  the neighbours, not the schools, and it correlates with household income at r=+0.87
  and with median sold price at r=+0.76. Using it as a school metric would be scoring
  a town's wealth twice and calling it education. THIS file is actual test results.
